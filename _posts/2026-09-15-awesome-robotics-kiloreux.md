[Awesome Robotics](https://github.com/kiloreux/awesome-robotics) is one of the longest-running "awesome list"-style directories in robotics, maintained by [kiloreux](https://github.com/kiloreux). It's a single, community-curated page that links out to courses, books, papers, software libraries, simulators, journals, competitions, conferences, and related lists — a good first stop when you want a broad map of what's out there.

---

## What's in it

**Courses.** University and MOOC offerings spanning ROS, mobile robotics, control, and manipulation — from MIT's Underactuated Robotics to ETH's Autonomous Mobile Robots and Penn's GRASP specialization.

**Books.** Foundational texts including *Probabilistic Robotics*, *Planning Algorithms*, *Modern Robotics*, and *A Mathematical Introduction to Robotic Manipulation* — several freely available online.

**Software and libraries.** Simulators and frameworks such as Gazebo, Webots, CoppeliaSim, Drake, and MRPT, plus utilities and ROS packages.

**Papers, journals, and conferences.** Links into the main robotics research venues (ICRA, IROS, RSS, HRI) and journals (IEEE T-RO, IJRR).

**Competitions, companies, and communities.** RoboCup, FIRST, DARPA challenges, major manufacturers, and community hubs like IEEE Spectrum Robotics and the robotics Stack Exchange.

**Related awesome lists.** Pointers to adjacent curated lists for computer vision, machine learning, reinforcement learning, and ROS 2.

---

## Why it's worth bookmarking

Its strength is breadth. Where more focused resources go deep on a single topic, this list gives you a wide, well-organized index to orient yourself — especially useful when you're new to an area and want to know which courses, books, and tools the community actually points to.

We've folded the most relevant, still-active entries into this site directly — free courses on the [Courses page]({{ site.baseurl }}/library/courses/), books, journals, software, and community links in the [References section]({{ site.baseurl }}/library/references/), and practical tutorial sites under [Tutorials]({{ site.baseurl }}/tutorials/). Pair it with the [Awesome Weekly Robotics]({{ site.baseurl }}/library/references/robotics/2026/08/05/awesome-weekly-robotics/) list, which leans more toward current open-source projects.
