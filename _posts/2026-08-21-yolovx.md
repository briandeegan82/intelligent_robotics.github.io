[YOLOvX](https://yolovx.com/) is a mobile app from [Wiserli](https://yolovx.com/) that lets you deploy, test, and share custom-trained computer vision models directly on a phone — no cloud round-trip required. It's a commercial product rather than an open-source project, but it fills a practical gap: quickly sanity-checking a trained detector on real-world footage before committing to a full embedded deployment.

---

## What it does

- **On-device inference** — models run locally on iOS and Android, so testing works without an internet connection or a server in the loop.
- **Bring your own model** — supports custom-trained models built on MobileNet, YOLOv3, YOLOv5, YOLOv8, and YOLOv9, with YOLOv10 support listed as forthcoming.
- **Real-time testing and sharing** — point the camera at a scene and see detections live, and share a working model with teammates without them needing to set up an inference environment themselves.
- **Benchmarking** — the site includes a benchmark/ranking section for comparing model performance.

Usage highlighted on the site spans battery sorting, agricultural drone testing, and sales demos — the common thread being people who need to show a model working in the field, on a phone, without lugging around a GPU.

---

## Why it's worth a look

Getting a trained YOLO model onto a phone for a quick field test is usually its own small project — export, convert, wire up a camera pipeline, build a minimal app. YOLOvX collapses that into "load your weights and point the camera," which is useful when you want fast, in-the-field feedback on a model rather than a full production edge deployment. Worth checking out if you're iterating on a detector and want to validate it on-device before investing in a bespoke mobile or embedded pipeline.
