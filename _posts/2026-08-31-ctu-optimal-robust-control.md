[Optimal and Robust Control (B3M35ORR, BE3M35ORR)](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLMLojHoA_QPmRiPotD_TnfdUkglTexuqm) is a graduate course at **Czech Technical University (CTU) in Prague**, taught by the [aa4cc](https://www.youtube.com/@aa4cc) group. The full lecture series is on YouTube and covers the mathematical foundations and design methods for optimal and robust control — the material you'd expect from a serious MSc/PhD-level controls curriculum rather than an introductory overview.

---

## What it covers

The playlist walks through classical and modern optimal control theory and robustness analysis — linear-quadratic methods, state-space design, and the tools needed to reason about performance and uncertainty in real systems. It's a natural follow-on if you've worked through introductory robotics control (e.g. manipulator dynamics and feedback) and want a deeper, theory-first treatment of how to **design** controllers rather than just tune them.

---

## Why it's worth bookmarking

University control courses at this level are often locked behind institutional access; CTU publishes the lectures openly. The pacing and notation are consistent across the series, so it works well as structured video coursework alongside a standard text like Åström & Murray or Boyd & Vandenberghe. If you're building up foundations for model predictive control, trajectory optimisation, or robust autonomy stacks, this is solid background material.
