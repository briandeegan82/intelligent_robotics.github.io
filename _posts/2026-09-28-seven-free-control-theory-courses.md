If you want to get serious about robotics, control theory is not optional. [Akshet Patel](https://www.linkedin.com/in/akshetpatel) put together a short list of **seven free resources** that carry you from classical control and state-space methods through nonlinear, optimal, and robust control.

Here is that list with direct links, plus how it maps onto this site's [Free University Courses]({{ site.baseurl }}/library/courses/) curriculum.

---

## The list

1. **[Control Bootcamp — Steve Brunton](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLMrJAkhIeNNR20Mz-VpzgfQs5zrYi085m)**  
   A fast, intuition-first tour of modern/optimal control: LQR, Kalman filtering, LQG, and related ideas with MATLAB examples.

2. **[Classical Control Theory — Brian Douglas](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLUMWjy5jgHK1NC52DXXrriwihVrYZKqjk)**  
   Clear, visual explanations of classical control: transfer functions, root locus, frequency response, and PID.

3. **[Control Systems Engineering — Benjamin Drew](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PL5105727DD6E8DE98)**  
   A structured run through control systems engineering fundamentals, good as a companion to a first controls textbook.

4. **[Nonlinear Control — MIT (Nonlinear Systems Laboratory)](http://web.mit.edu/nsl/www/videos/lectures.html)**  
   Lecture videos from Jean-Jacques Slotine's Nonlinear Systems Laboratory at MIT, covering nonlinear control and dynamics.

5. **[Numerical Methods for Optimal Control — NTNU](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLc2vvxBHfBcrzR8fhWc7qjT1lr51Kjue2)**  
   Numerical approaches to optimal control problems — the computational backbone behind trajectory optimisation and MPC.

6. **[Optimal and Robust Control — CTU Prague](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLMLojHoA_QPmRiPotD_TnfdUkglTexuqm)**  
   Graduate-level treatment of optimal control design and robustness analysis. See this site's [full write-up]({{ site.baseurl }}/library/courses/robotics/2026/08/31/ctu-optimal-robust-control/) for more detail.

7. **[State Space Control — University of Utah](https://www.telerobotics.utah.edu/index.php/StateSpaceControl)**  
   State-space modelling and control design, from the Telerobotics Lab at the University of Utah.

## How to use the list

Together, these courses move from classical control and state-space methods to nonlinear, optimal, and robust control:

| Stage | Focus | Courses |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Classical control | Classical Control Theory, Control Systems Engineering |
| 2 | State-space and modern control | Control Bootcamp, State Space Control |
| 3 | Nonlinear, optimal, and robust control | Nonlinear Control, Numerical Methods for Optimal Control, Optimal and Robust Control |

Save this list if you are studying robotics, autonomous systems, or control engineering. For a broader, topic-organised directory (Michigan, MIT, Stanford, ETH, NPTEL, and more), use the [Free University Courses]({{ site.baseurl }}/library/courses/) page.
