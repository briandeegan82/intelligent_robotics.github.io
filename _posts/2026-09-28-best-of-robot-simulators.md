[Best-of Robot Simulators](https://github.com/knmcguire/best-of-robot-simulators) is a ranked "best-of" directory of robotics simulation projects, maintained by [Kimberly McGuire](https://github.com/knmcguire). It currently indexes around 180 projects across 11 categories, scored from GitHub and package-manager metrics — a useful map when you're choosing (or comparing) simulators rather than hunting for one name you already know.

---

## What's in it

**Generic robotics simulators.** Full stacks such as MuJoCo, Gazebo, Webots, Newton, Unity Robotics Hub, and O3DE for Robotics.

**Domain-specific simulators.** Separate sections for aerial, maritime, automotive, and space robotics, plus 2D simulators and other specialised domains.

**AI training simulators.** Environments aimed at reinforcement learning and large-scale data generation.

**Underlying engines.** Physics engines, rendering engines, and game engines that robotics simulators build on.

Projects are ranked with a quality score (stars, activity, contributors, and related signals), so the list is oriented toward discovery and comparison, not just alphabetical browsing.

---

## Why it's worth bookmarking

Most "awesome lists" for robotics are broad. This one is narrow and deep on simulation: if you need to pick a tool for ROS integration, contact-rich RL, photorealistic sensors, or a domain like drones or underwater robots, the category layout and ranking make that faster. Pair it with this site's [simulation tutorial overview]({{ site.baseurl }}/tutorials/simulation/2025/06/08/robotics-simulators-overview/) for narrative guidance on Gazebo, MuJoCo, Webots, Unity, and Isaac Sim.

Note: automated weekly regenerations are currently paused (see the [repo issue](https://github.com/knmcguire/best-of-robot-simulators/issues/275)); the list itself remains a strong reference.
