The Soft Robotics Lab (SRL) at ETH Zurich announced a guest lecture on **reinforcement learning and dexterous manipulation**, part of the university's Real World Robotics course.

---

## Details

- **Speaker:** Kaifeng Zhang, Sharpa
- **When:** Monday, 26 October 2026, 14:00-15:00 CET (already held at the time of writing)
- **Topics:** choosing RL algorithms, practical training methods, and current research applications in robotics
- **Course context:** students are applying RL on robotic hands they built themselves

---

## Why it's worth a look

The course pairs hands-on hardware with RL training, which is a useful model for teaching dexterous manipulation. The post doesn't say whether a recording will be shared, so check the SRL channels if you want the material.
