Lukas M. Ziegler highlighted that Seeed Studio has released **reBot-DevArm**, a fully open-source desktop robotic arm aimed at people learning robotics.

---

## What's in the release

- **Hardware:** sheet metal and 3D-printed parts, with a detailed bill of materials and CAD files published openly
- **Software:** open-source, with a Python SDK
- **Ecosystem:** ROS1/ROS2, Isaac Sim and LeRobot are listed

---

## Specifications (as posted)

| Spec | Value |
|---|---|
| Degrees of freedom | 6 DoF plus gripper |
| Payload | 1.5 kg |
| Max reach | 650 mm |
| Weight | 4.5 kg |
| Repeatability | under 0.2 mm |

---

## Why it's worth a look

An arm with published CAD, a BOM and ROS/LeRobot support gives students and hobbyists a platform they can build, modify and use for imitation learning without a vendor lock-in. I haven't built one, so check the repository for license terms, the real cost of the BOM, and how complete the software support is.
