Anuj Gupta, CEO of SimForge, announced that [SimForge Studio](https://simforge.ai) is open for autonomy development.

---

## What's in the release

- **Data:** seven maps and digital twins, with nearly 25,000 driving scenarios in the research release
- **Rendering and simulation:** SimForge Helios, a renderer and simulation engine that can run on your own infrastructure
- **Evaluation:** open- and closed-loop workflows with Alpamayo 1.5 Nano, Alpamayo 2 Super, Qwen Drive and SimForge Pilot-4B V1.5
- **Training and data generation:** SimForge Gym for RL policy training, plus scenario generation and rendering tools for SFT data
- **Access:** through AI agents, a CLI and Python toolkit, or the Studio web UI

---

## Licensing

The research release is available for non-commercial use under the applicable licenses. Commercial licensing is sold separately.

---

## Why it's worth a look

Closed-loop evaluation of driving models usually needs a simulator and scenario set you build yourself. A packaged set of maps, scenarios and evaluation workflows, with a renderer you can run on your own hardware, lowers that barrier for research. I haven't tried it, so check the license terms and what the research release includes.
