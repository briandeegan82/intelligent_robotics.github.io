> **Updated:** Individual course listings from this round-up have been absorbed into the structured **[Free University Courses]({{ site.baseurl }}/library/courses/)** curriculum (Michigan, MIT, Stanford, ETH, NPTEL, and additional open lectures). Use that page as the canonical directory.

One of the best-kept secrets in robotics education is how much world-class lecture material universities publish openly. This post remains as a short companion spotlight; the full topic-by-topic curriculum, free-access legend, suggested path, and top-15 picks live on the Library courses page.

## Where to start

1. **[Free University Courses]({{ site.baseurl }}/library/courses/)** — structured curriculum with free/open vs MOOC labelling  
2. **[Stanford CS223A](https://see.stanford.edu/Course/CS223A)** — classical robotics foundation  
3. **[MIT 2.12](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/2-12-introduction-to-robotics-fall-2005/)** — broad robotics engineering  
4. **[Michigan Robotics online courses](https://robotics.umich.edu/academics/courses/online-courses/)** — multi-course free sequence  
5. **[ETH RSL lectures](https://rsl.ethz.ch/education-students/lectures.html)** — ROS and dynamics
