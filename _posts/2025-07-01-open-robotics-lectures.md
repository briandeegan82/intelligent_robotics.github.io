One of the best-kept secrets in robotics education is just how much world-class lecture material has been made freely available online by leading university programmes. Whether you are a graduate student filling gaps in your foundations, a researcher branching into an adjacent area, or a practitioner who learns better from video than from papers, this list is for you.

The courses below span the full breadth of modern robotics — from classical kinematics and dynamics through to learned manipulation policies, legged locomotion, and autonomous driving perception. Where possible, direct links to video playlists, course websites, and lecture notes are included. All material listed here is openly accessible without institutional login.

---

## Foundations & Classical Robotics

### Stanford CS223A — Introduction to Robotics
**Instructor:** Prof. Oussama Khatib  
**Format:** Full lecture videos (recorded), lecture notes PDF  
**Topics:** Spatial descriptions, kinematics, Jacobians, statics, dynamics, trajectory generation, motion planning, force control  
**Link:** [Stanford Engineering Everywhere (SEE)](https://see.stanford.edu/Course/CS223A) · [YouTube playlist via Class Central](https://www.classcentral.com/course/youtube-lecture-collection-introduction-to-robotics-54773)

The canonical entry point for robot manipulator theory. Khatib's lectures cover the mathematical modelling of robotic systems with rigour, progressing from geometric foundations through dynamics and control. The accompanying lecture notes (Khatib & Kolarov) are widely used as a standalone reference.

---

### Università di Roma — Robotics 1 & Robotics 2
**Instructor:** Prof. Alessandro De Luca  
**Format:** Full lecture video series on YouTube  
**Topics (Robotics 1):** DH parameters, forward/inverse kinematics, velocity kinematics, workspace analysis, trajectory planning  
**Topics (Robotics 2):** Dynamic modelling, feedback linearisation, redundancy resolution, nonlinear control  
**Link:** [Robotics 1 – YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLAQopGWlIcyaqDBW1zSKx7lHfVcOmWSWt) · [Robotics 2 – YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLAQopGWlIcybLDFpBJ7OyvPFgc4LDSIJM)

De Luca's two-course sequence is among the most thorough freely available treatments of classical robotics. The presentation is mathematically careful and well-paced; Robotics 2 in particular is an excellent complement to Spong or Siciliano as a primary text.

---

### Seoul National University — Robot Mechanics and Control
**Instructor:** Prof. Frank Park  
**Format:** Video lectures  
**Topics:** Lie group methods, screw theory, modern robotics (linked to the Lynch & Park textbook)  
**Link:** [YouTube – SNU Robot Mechanics and Control](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLkjv97Z8XejfCKaHHMRNFZJiRy0LO9Scs)

Accompanies the open-access textbook *Modern Robotics: Mechanics, Planning, and Control* (Lynch & Park, Cambridge University Press). The textbook PDF and supplementary materials are freely available from the [course website](http://modernrobotics.org).

## MIT OpenCourseWare Highlights

### MIT 6.832 — Underactuated Robotics
**Instructor:** Prof. Russ Tedrake  
**Format:** Lecture videos (YouTube), interactive HTML lecture notes, problem sets  
**Topics:** Nonlinear dynamics, passive walkers, energy-shaping control, trajectory optimisation, reinforcement learning for locomotion, manipulation  
**Link:** [underactuated.csail.mit.edu](https://underactuated.csail.mit.edu) · [YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/@underactuatedrobotics) · [MIT OCW](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/6-832-underactuated-robotics-spring-2009/)

A landmark course. Tedrake's lecture notes are continuously updated and combine theory with interactive Python/Drake examples. Lecture videos from multiple years (2018, 2022, 2024) are on YouTube. Essential reading for anyone working on legged robots, aerial vehicles, or learning-based control.

---

### MIT 6.801 — Machine Vision
**Instructor:** Prof. Berthold Horn  
**Format:** Video lectures (YouTube, Fall 2020)  
**Topics:** Image formation, photometry, feature extraction, stereo, optical flow, shape from shading, object recognition  
**Link:** [YouTube playlist](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLUl4u3cNGP63pfpS1gV5P9tDxxL_e4W8O)

A rigorous treatment of the geometric and photometric foundations of computer vision with strong relevance to robotic perception pipelines.

### CMU 16-745 — Optimal Control and Reinforcement Learning
**Year:** 2024 (notebooks + video)  
**Format:** Lecture videos (YouTube), Jupyter notebooks  
**Topics:** LQR, iLQR, model predictive control, trajectory optimisation, policy gradient methods  
**Link:** [YouTube 2024](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLZnJoM76RM6KgchA1BI7DXQBN5T8daTOy) · [YouTube 2023](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLZnJoM76RM6IXN0iNgZWxiAHHlpBMBGHl)

Tedrake-style rigour applied to optimal control, taught at CMU. The 2024 recitation recordings are separately available and work through the numerical methods hands-on.

## UC Berkeley

### CS287 — Advanced Robotics
**Instructor:** Prof. Pieter Abbeel  
**Format:** Lecture videos  
**Topics:** MDPs, POMDPs, motion planning, probabilistic inference, manipulation, robot learning  
**Link:** [Fall 2019 course page](https://people.eecs.berkeley.edu/~pabbeel/cs287-fa19/) · [Fall 2019 YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLwRJQ4m4UJjNBPJdt8WamRAt4XKc639wF)

Abbeel's advanced course sits at the intersection of classical robotics and modern ML. Suitable for students who have a solid linear algebra and probability background and want to understand planning, learning from demonstration, and reinforcement learning in a robotics context.

---

## ETH Zürich

### Autonomous Mobile Robots
**Instructors:** Roland Siegwart, Davide Scaramuzza, Marco Hutter et al.  
**Format:** MOOC-style video lectures (edX / Class Central)  
**Topics:** Wheeled kinematics, environment perception, probabilistic localisation, mapping, SLAM, motion planning, drones, legged robots  
**Link:** [Class Central listing](https://www.classcentral.com/course/autonomous-robotics-eth-zurich-autonomous-mobile--1564)

Tightly coupled to the widely used textbook *Introduction to Autonomous Mobile Robots* (Siegwart, Nourbakhsh, Scaramuzza). Covers the breadth of mobile autonomy from fundamentals through to multi-robot systems.

---

### Robot Learning (ETH CVG)
**Instructor:** Prof. Oier Mees  
**Format:** Lecture notes, assignments  
**Topics:** Imitation learning, reinforcement learning, policy optimisation, Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models, foundation models for robotics  
**Link:** [cvg.ethz.ch/lectures/Robot-Learning](https://cvg.ethz.ch/lectures/Robot-Learning/)

A 2026-edition course covering the full modern robot learning stack, from basic behaviour cloning through to large-scale VLA models. Course materials and a GitHub repo are publicly accessible.

---

## University of Waterloo

### ME 597 — Autonomous Mobile Robotics (Fall 2014)
**Instructor:** Prof. Timothy Barfoot  
**Format:** Lecture videos  
**Topics:** State estimation, EKF/UKF, particle filters, visual odometry, SLAM, GPS-denied navigation  
**Link:** [YouTube playlist](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLEQvNLZXEcNEPySeXxiVTlv73T8BXNQZJ)

### ME 780 — Perception for Autonomous Driving (Spring 2017)
**Instructor:** Prof. Steven Waslander  
**Format:** Lecture videos  
**Topics:** Sensor modalities (camera, LiDAR, radar), calibration, depth estimation, object detection, sensor fusion  
**Link:** [YouTube playlist](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLEQvNLZXEcNER_f7CKT-M8MnRiG0PXFMQ)

### ME 780 — Nonlinear State Estimation for Robotics and Computer Vision (Spring 2017)
**Format:** Lecture videos  
**Topics:** Lie groups, SE(3) estimation, batch and incremental optimisation, factor graphs  
**Link:** [YouTube playlist](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLEQvNLZXEcNER_f7CKT-M8MnRiG0PXFMQ)

Barfoot and Waslander's Waterloo courses are particularly relevant for autonomous vehicle and outdoor robotics research. Barfoot's material closely follows his book *State Estimation for Robotics* (Cambridge, open draft available).

---

## TU Munich

### Visual Navigation for Flying Robots
**Format:** Lecture videos (YouTube)  
**Topics:** Visual odometry, dense mapping, optical flow, keyframe-based SLAM, quadrotor control  
**Link:** [YouTube playlist](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLTBdjV_4f-EKeki5ps2WHqJqyQvxls4ha)

A practical course on vision-based navigation for aerial platforms. Covers the algorithmic pipeline from raw imagery through to state estimation and control, with strong ties to LSD-SLAM and DSO.

## University of Queensland

### METR 4202/7202 — Robotics & Automation
**Format:** Lecture videos  
**Topics:** Robot kinematics and dynamics, Denavit-Hartenberg, control, computer vision for robotics, path planning  
**Link:** [YouTube playlist](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLyPiP9VCZmVCLMnnbJRLxjmhHmBH7MVKP)
