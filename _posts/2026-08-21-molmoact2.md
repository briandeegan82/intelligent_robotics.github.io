[MolmoAct2](https://allenai.org/blog/molmoact2) is Ai2's second-generation Action Reasoning Model (ARM) — a robotics foundation model that reasons about its surroundings in 3D before acting, rather than mapping pixels straight to actions. The full stack is open: paper, weights, training/deployment code, and datasets are all public.

---

## What's new versus MolmoAct

MolmoAct2 pairs **Molmo 2-ER**, an embodied-reasoning vision-language backbone trained on ~3M embodied-reasoning examples, with a dedicated action expert that uses flow matching, joined by a KV-cache bridge. It adds an open-source FAST tokenizer reimplementation and an adaptive-depth mechanism for 3D reasoning, and ships with bimanual manipulation built into the base model rather than bolted on.

The headline numbers:

- **~37x faster inference** — base model actions run in ~180ms versus 6,700ms for the original MolmoAct.
- **LIBERO benchmark**: 97.2–98.1% success post-training, up from ~87% for MolmoAct.
- **Zero-shot real-world (Franka)**: 87.1% average success versus 48.4% for the original.
- Ranked first among bimanual policies in third-party evaluation by Cortex AI (0.51 average score).

## Why it's worth a look

The repo covers the full loop — training, fine-tuning, evaluation, and deployment — with FastAPI inference servers already wired up for DROID and Bimanual YAM setups behind a unified `/act` protocol. The included **MolmoAct2-BimanualYAM dataset** is billed as the largest open-source bimanual robotics dataset released to date, at over 720 hours of demonstrations. Combined with native LeRobot integration and Apache 2.0 licensing, it's a solid base for anyone fine-tuning a VLA for a real arm rather than just benchmarking in simulation.
